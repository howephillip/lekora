# Voice (Phase 4)

Talk to your agents and hear them back. Three features, built and confirmed in this order: **voice notes**, **hearing replies**, **hands-free conversation**.

Owner's decisions (2026-11-11): spoken replies use iPhone voices by default (instant, sentence by sentence) with a per-agent option to use Hermes' own voice when a cloud voice is set up there; voice note transcripts show in small print under the voice bubble, like Messages; hands-free is built in this phase.

## 1. Voice notes
- **Recording** (as in Messages; owner's request 2026-11-11): a waveform button inside the message bar, on the right, while nothing is typed (typing turns it into Send). Hold it for half a second (a red ring fills, so a stray tap does nothing); recording starts with a haptic and you can let go and talk. The bar becomes a red live waveform with the time and a stop button. Stopping (light haptic) turns the bar, with a spring animation, into: **×** on the far left to throw it away; in the bar, play it back (drag the waveform to skip), **+ 0:07** records more onto the end, and Send. Up to 10 minutes.
- **In the chat:** a voice bubble with play/pause, a waveform you can drag to skip around, and the length. Under it, in small print, what Hermes heard (its transcript), once it arrives.
- **How:** the recording (AAC in .m4a) is uploaded like any file (`docs/files.md`) and sent with `voice` details (length, waveform). The plugin hands it to Hermes as a **voice message** (`MessageType.VOICE`), so Hermes transcribes it with its own speech-to-text (by default a free local Whisper, set up by Hermes on first use; or whatever `stt.provider` is set to) and the agent answers the words. Hermes posts the transcript back (`🎙️ "…"`, its `stt.echo_transcripts` setting, on by default); the plugin attaches it to the voice message instead of showing it as a separate line.
- If Hermes can't transcribe it, the agent still gets the recording's location (Hermes' own behaviour) and no transcript shows.

## 2. Hearing replies
Owner's feedback (2026-11-11): Hermes' voice must work with any voice a user has set up in Hermes (built in, a command provider or a plugin one), not a fixed list; voice mode must deliver replies as voice notes in that native voice, streamed, with a quick switch in the chat. (2026-11-12): it must be seamless (never text first, then swapped), play reliably to the end, and offer a **personality voice**: the reply turned into an expressive voice script with the user's own instructions before it's spoken; personality voice notes are burnt orange.

- **Voice mode** (Chat Settings › Voice › **Voice Replies**: Off / To My Voice Notes / Always; **Style**: Voice / Personality Voice; and the **waveform button** at the top of the chat: tap = on/off, hold = choose Off / Voice / Personality Voice):
  - **Decided at the start of each reply.** The plugin decides when the reply begins (first streamed words, or before saving a reply that wasn't streamed) and tells the phones at once (`voice.reply`); the saved message carries `voicing`. The phone shows a voice note from the first word (spinner and shimmering waveform until the first audio, the text filling in underneath), never a text bubble.
  - **Voice:** finished sentences (very short ones joined to the next) are spoken with Hermes' `text_to_speech` in the agent's own voice and sent as they're made (`voice.segment`); the phone fetches and plays them strictly in order, each once, to the end of the reply. When it's saved and spoken, the parts are attached (`spoken`) and it stays a voice note (play, drag to skip, real waveform and length) with its text as the transcript.
  - **Personality Voice:** the agent itself writes its reply the way it talks, and exactly that is spoken. Burnt-orange voice notes. Per agent, Chat Settings › Voice › **Voice Personality**:
    - **Who's speaking:** the agent as itself (its own Hermes persona); one of Hermes' **personalities** (catgirl, helpful, hype… whatever that agent's Hermes lists; described to the agent); or **My Own** (written in the app). Plus **Cadence**: Filler Words None / Some / Lots ("uh", "um", "like", "you know"), Pauses Few / Natural / Lots, Pace Calm / Natural / Lively; and built-in speech rules (contractions, varied sentences, the odd restart, numbers said out, delivery tags like `[laughs]` inline where the voice understands them; no Markdown).
    - **Priority:** a custom or Hermes personality comes first in the instruction and takes priority over the agent's usual tone, style and wording rules (its persona file, skills) for that chat, while its knowledge, memory, facts and tools stay as they are. Lekora filters nothing; the model or its provider may still hold some things back.
    - **How:** for each message you send while it's on, the plugin passes Hermes' own per-turn chat instruction (`MessageEvent.channel_prompt`: part of the agent's system prompt for that turn, not your message) saying the reply will be spoken and how to sound. AGENTS.md rule 7 exception, owner 2026-11-12.
    - **Why not a hidden rewrite:** the first version rewrote replies into speech with a second model call. On 2026-11-12 that rewrite made up a story (a bar incident) the agent had never said, and the agent couldn't follow up because it only knew its written reply. Now what you hear, what you read and what the agent remembers are the same, and there's no second call.
  - **Transcripts are compact:** two lines under the voice note with a chevron; tap to read it all (owner, 2026-11-12).
  - **Audio the iPhone can play:** Hermes voices can produce Ogg/Opus and other formats iPhone can't play. The plugin checks each file's real format and converts it with ffmpeg when the Hermes machine has it; if not, the reply shows as text with a plain explanation ("install ffmpeg or set the voice's output_format to mp3"), instead of a silent "playing" bubble. If the phone still can't play a file, the bubble says so.
  - Without a Hermes voice: the iPhone voice reads the reply aloud as it's written (and, once it starts, to the end); the reply stays text.
  - Turning it off keeps existing voice notes; the next replies come as text with the Listen button. "To My Voice Notes": only replies to a voice note you sent.
- **Listen:** the speaker under a text reply. iPhone voice by default (sentence by sentence; voice and speed per agent, every iPhone voice, Personal Voice), or **Listen With Hermes' Voice** per agent (all of Hermes' audio parts, in order).
- **Hermes' voice is offered** whenever Hermes can speak with that agent's settings (`check_tts_requirements` in the agent's profile, with its keys; checked at most once a minute per agent). A failed attempt is retried once and logged in Hermes' log.
- **Where settings live:** the plugin's store (`app_settings`, per agent), so every phone and a new phone get them (rule 3); other phones update straight away. Nothing is written to Hermes' config.

## 3. Hands-free conversation
Owner's choices (2026-11-12): full-screen like a call; talking over the agent interrupts it; keeps going with the screen locked (background audio). Plus: a Voice / Personality switch on the call screen, and (for speed) the iPhone's own speech recognition instead of uploading a recording for Hermes to transcribe.

- **Start:** the headphones button at the top of a chat. Full screen: the agent's face with rings that move with your voice, its status (Listening… / Thinking… / Speaking… talk to interrupt / Muted), **Voice | Personality** (the agent's Hermes voice, plain or personality), **Mute** and **End**.
- **Your turn:** the app listens continuously (microphone with the iPhone's echo cancellation, so it doesn't hear the agent). It measures the room for the first two seconds, then notices talking (well above the background noise, which it keeps adapting to) and the end of it (about a second of quiet; one turn at most a minute).
- **Words, not loudness, decide.** The iPhone's on-device speech recognition (Apple's SpeechTranscriber, or the dictation recognizer on iPhones without it) transcribes as you talk; when your turn ends, the **words are sent straight to the agent** as a message (no upload, no waiting for Hermes' speech-to-text). A turn with no words (a cough, a door, background noise) is never sent. Talking over the agent stops it only once words are heard.
- Without on-device recognition (some older iPhones, the simulator): the recording is sent as a voice note for Hermes to transcribe, and only if it's long enough and mostly sound.
- **The agent's turn:** messages sent hands-free always get a spoken reply (`hands_free` on `chat.send`): the agent's Hermes voice, plain or personality (voice notes streamed as in §2), or the iPhone voice if it has none. When it's done, the app listens again. Everything stays in the chat as normal messages and voice notes.
- **Background:** the app declares background audio, so the conversation goes on with the screen locked; iOS shows its microphone indicator. It stops when you tap End or leave the chat.
- **No transcript on the call screen** (owner, 2026-11-12): what was said lives in the chat history.
- **The vibe** (owner, 2026-11-12: "wish the user's laughter could be understood"): alongside the words, Apple's built-in sound recognition (SoundAnalysis, on the iPhone, free; no paid service such as Fish) listens for laughing, giggling, chuckling, sighs, gasps, crying, whispering, shouting, singing, humming, whistling and clapping. Each one heard with enough confidence is written into your words where it happened, e.g. "that's hilarious [laughs] okay so…", so the agent knows how it was said. Sounds go only with words (a laugh alone is never a turn, and never interrupts). Coughs and sneezes are left out.
- **Speed** (owner, 2026-11-12: 5–10 s from speaking to the reply): every hands-free turn writes one line to the Hermes log (`Lekora hands-free timing`): waiting for quiet, recognition, the agent's first words, its first sentence, making the voice, sending it, the phone receiving and starting it, and the total from your last word to the first sound. Quicker already: the end of your turn after 0.9 s of quiet (was 1.2 s; the words tell a turn from noise); the reply's first sentence is spoken at once however short (later short ones still join the next); a small first voice part travels with its `voice.segment` so the phone plays it without fetching it.
- Tests: `HandsFreeTests` (turns start and end on talking and quiet, coughs and blips aren't turns, noise raises the bar, noise that starts later stops counting, a long stretch of talking stays one turn); sounds woven in by time, once each, only meaningful ones; plugin `test_hands_free_replies_are_always_spoken`, `test_timings_are_logged_for_the_first_sound_and_the_phones_half`, first sentence at once and inline audio (`test_a_streamed_reply_is_a_voice_note_from_the_first_word`); app inline audio kept as a fetched file (`HearingRepliesTests`); simulator: the call screen, mute, style switch, end, 110 s of silence sends nothing, a spoken phrase (the Mac's voice) goes through and is answered.
